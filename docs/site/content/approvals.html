Review a request

An approval asks you to decide whether a specific action may proceed. Open the request from Chat or Inbox. Review the action, affected workspace or destination, and risk before deciding.

Use Approve once when you want to authorize the pending action. Choose Deny to decline. Some requests open a further review with higher-risk confirmation.

Temporary grants

Eligible tool approvals may offer a temporary allowance. Pick a duration from the options the request offers and review its scope before confirming. The allowance expires and can be revoked. Current policy still applies on every use.

Confirm the outcome

After deciding, return to Chat or the linked run. Check the recorded result to see whether execution succeeded, failed, or still needs attention. The approval record shows your decision. The run record shows execution.

For the relationship between decisions and run state, see the approval state model.
