# Meeting notes check

Pack-Version: Meeting notes check 1.0

Two chat tests for the meeting-notes skill. They check that tidied notes keep what was decided and do not invent what was not said.

## Chat

### TEST-01: Keeps the decisions

Tidy these meeting notes into a summary, decisions, action items and open questions.

Notes: We met on Tuesday about the website move. Dana said the old site should go offline on the first of the month. Everyone agreed. Priya will ask the host about the redirect rules. Sam wondered whether the newsletter links will break, and nobody had an answer.

### TEST-02: Leaves gaps visible

Tidy these meeting notes. Where the notes do not say who owns something or when it is due, say so.

Notes: Budget review. We need a new laptop for the design team and the quote should be checked again. Also someone should tell finance about the extra licences. Next meeting is probably in two weeks.
