Whether you operate digital wallets, BNPL platforms, payment of APIs, embedded finance products, or SaaS infrastructure that touches transactions, card data inevitably enters your environment—even if briefly. PCI DSS applies the moment cardholder data is stored, processed, or transmitted, directly or indirectly.
